## Brand & Style

The brand philosophy balances clinical authority with deep emotional empathy. Designed specifically for adult oncology patients, family caregivers, and clinical teams, the experience counters the sterile anxiety of standard medical software through an approach rooted in dignity, calm, and clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Sober & Reassuring:** Grounded, unhurried, and medically dependable. Avoids clinical coldness as well as patronizing or naive cheerfulness.
- **Supportive & Accessible:** Engineered for users managing physical fatigue, "chemo brain," or sensory overload. Every visual choice reduces cognitive burden.
- **Discreet & Compassionate:** Respectful of personal boundaries and life circumstances.

### Design Movement
- **Modern Humanist Minimalist with Tactile Warmth:** Generous whitespace, organic rounded surfaces, rich low-contrast layering, and warm-tinted neutral substrates. It strips away technical clutter to let treatment steps, symptom reporting, and vital metrics breathe naturally.

## Colors

The palette avoids cold hospital whites and sharp corporate blues. Instead, it relies on mineral teals, muted slates, restorative herbal greens, and warm linen backdrops.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#134e54` - Deep Petroleum Teal):** Represents anchored clinical competence, safety, and focused guidance. Used for core interactive items, major navigation, and authoritative titles.
- **Secondary (`#6b6e82` - Heather Slate / Muted Lavender-Gray):** Bridges clinical rigor and comforting composure. Used for supportive actions, neutral categories, and subdued interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#3d8573` - Restorative Sage Mint):** Conveys stability, cellular recovery, healthy metric thresholds, and completed milestones.
- **Neutral Backgrounds & Surfaces (`#f8f7f5` - Warm Linen / Rice Paper):** Prevents screen glare, reduces eye fatigue, and replaces sterile white tones. Secondary containers rest on `#f0eee9`, while borders adopt an organic `#e4e0d8`.
- **Functional Accents (Semantic):**
  - **Amber / Calming Coral (`#c85a32` & `#b87428`):** Flags medication alerts, pain spike notifications, and urgent care protocols with visibility that commands attention without inducing panic.
  - **Neutral Dark (`#1a2426`):** Off-black mineral slate for body typography, maximizing legibility without the harsh contrast of `#000000`.

## Typography

The type hierarchy prioritizes effortless scanning and high legibility during periods of mental fatigue. `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric balance softened by open counters and humanized terminals.

### Hierarchy & Scale Guidelines
- **Comfortable Reading Metrics:** Body text maintains a minimum base of `15px` to ensure effortless legibility under altered motor or visual conditions.
- **Line Height Ratios:** Generous line heights (`1.45` to `1.55` on body copy) prevent lines from blurring together during long reading sessions.
- **Tracking & Weight Strategy:** Headlines favor `SemiBold` (`600`) over heavy black weights to avoid an aggressive tone, while maintaining strong visual contrast against body text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around relaxed interaction zones, high hit-target tolerance, and rhythmic vertical progressions that preserve clarity and prevent choice overload.

### Layout Model
- **Grid Architecture:** 4-column fluid layout on mobile viewports (`360px`–`428px`) transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets (`>768px`). Outer screen padding is fixed at `20px` to keep elements away from hardware bezels.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Strictly adheres to an `8px` baseline grid. Content sections and cards are separated by `24px` margins to avoid visual crowding.
- **Motor Impairment Safeguards:** All interactive tap areas have a mandatory minimum bounding box of `48px × 48px`, expanding to `56px` for primary actions (such as logging symptoms or confirming daily medications).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, ambient tonal layering rather than high-contrast structural borders or harsh dropshadows.

### Shadow Tones & Direction
- **Subtle Surface Tinting:** Shadows use low-opacity petroleum gray (`rgba(19, 78, 84, 0.06)`) rather than synthetic gray/black tints, making cards look grounded on the warm linen base.
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Direct `#f8f7f5` page substrate.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Raised by `#ffffff` surfaces paired with `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(19, 78, 84, 0.05)` and an ultra-subtle border stroke (`1px solid rgba(228, 224, 216, 0.7)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Snackbars & Drawers):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(19, 78, 84, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Emergency Modals & Action Sheets):** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(26, 36, 38, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape language uses warm, organic geometry with rounded corners that soften the interface and feel approachable to the touch.

### Application Rules
- **Base Rounding (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Internal elements, small metric badges, and secondary input fields.
- **Intermediate Rounding (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Standard interactive controls, primary buttons, medication checklists, and chips.
- **Primary Shell Rounding (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Content cards, symptom tracking panels, bottom sheets, and summary containers.
- **Pill Formats (`rounded-full`):** Status indicators, vital sign pills, and floating action prompts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep petroleum teal (`#134e54`) fill with clean white text (`#ffffff`), `56px` height, `rounded-xl` (16px radius). Generates a subtle tactile press state (`scale(0.98)` with `#0c363a` fill).
- **Secondary Button:** Warm stone tinted background (`#eeebe5`) with petroleum teal text (`#134e54`), no harsh border, `48px` height.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with `#134e54` text and an underline offset of `4px`.
- **Alert / SOS Action:** Calming coral background (`#c85a32`) with white text, designed with high contrast for emergency oncological triage.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) background over the `#f8f7f5` canvas, `24px` rounded corners, `20px` internal padding.
- **Grouping:** Group complex clinical information (such as blood cell counts or infusion dates) with clear title hierarchy and soft divider lines (`#f0eee9`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** `52px` field height, `#ffffff` surface, `1.5px` border in `#dcd7cd`, transitioning to `#134e54` on focus. Helper text remains consistently visible below the field rather than hidden behind tooltips.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Expanded `24px × 24px` touch targets with `8px` corner radius for checkboxes. Active state uses `#134e54` fill with a bold white check mark.

### Chips & Filters
- **Resting:** Warm neutral fill (`#f0eee9`) with slate-slate text (`#454b4d`), `36px` height, `18px` pill radius.
- **Selected:** Soft petroleum teal tint (`#e6eff0`) with dark teal text (`#134e54`) and a subtle `1px` border in `#94bdc1`.

### Healthcare-Specific Components
- **Daily Symptom & Pain Scale Slider:** Thick `12px` progress track with distinct tactile milestones (1 through 10), paired with clear verbal descriptors ("Leve", "Moderado", "Intenso") alongside numeric values.
- **Medication Schedule Tile:** Includes a distinct visual icon, administration instructions ("Tomar com água após o café"), an easy-tap toggle state, and a countdown timer.
- **Care Team Quick-Contact Sheet:** Persistent, bottom-anchored modal providing direct access to the oncology nurse navigator and emergency room protocols with zero navigation friction.
